# DAYBAGGER — STRATEGY & GOLDEN RULES

## Core Objective

Build wealth through Indian equity intraday trading using technology, broad intelligence, predictive analytics and continuous learning—not fixed indicator rules or manual-style trading.

## Strategy

Daybagger is a **multi-source Indian Equity Meta-Intelligence Engine**.

It will:

* Scan the liquid Indian equity market broadly.
* Combine market, sector, stock, institutional, F&O/OI, volatility, global, news/event, historical and microstructure intelligence.
* Use multiple proven specialist strategies/models instead of relying on one strategy.
* Detect the current market regime and determine which strategies/signals are most reliable in that regime.
* Predict and rank opportunities by **probability, expected net return, risk and execution quality**.
* Allocate capital only to the best opportunities.
* Learn from trades, rejected opportunities and changing market behaviour.

## Golden Rules

1. **India First**
   Everything must be designed and validated specifically for NSE/Indian market behaviour.

2. **Equity Only for V1**
   Daybagger trades cash equities. Other markets, indices, F&O/OI, VIX, global markets, etc. may be used freely as intelligence inputs.

3. **No Intelligence Restrictions**
   Use any lawful information that improves prediction or execution: price, volume, breadth, sectors, NIFTY, VIX, institutional activity, F&O/OI, global cues, GIFT NIFTY, news, events, corporate actions, order book, historical analogues, expert strategies and quantitative models.

4. **Technology Must Create the Edge**
   Exploit capabilities humans cannot efficiently replicate: market-wide simultaneous scanning, historical comparison, regime detection, cross-sectional ranking, predictive probability, rapid information fusion and automated learning.

5. **No Single-Strategy Dependency**
   Momentum, pullback, relative strength, catalyst/news, sector rotation, mean reversion, microstructure and other validated strategies are specialist inputs. Daybagger decides which strategy deserves capital under current conditions.

6. **Evidence Over Indicators**
   No RSI/MACD/ADX/RVOL stacking or arbitrary confidence scores. Every meaningful metric, formula, filter or strategy must prove usefulness on Indian historical and out-of-sample data.

7. **Same Logic Everywhere**
   Live trading, replay, backtest and learning must use the same strategy implementation. No separate “backtest logic” and “live logic.”

8. **No Fake Data or Fake Evidence**
   Missing or invalid information means `NO TRADE / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`. Never manufacture candles, bid/ask, win rates, confirmations or backtest results.

9. **Adaptive, Not Unstable**
   Daybagger learns which signals work by regime, sector, time and event type. Weak signals are down-weighted; stronger ones gain influence. Production changes occur only after replay/out-of-sample validation.

10. **Wealth Building, Not Daily Gambling**
    Starting capital ≈ ₹30,000. Profit/loss targets are operating objectives, not reasons to force trades. Losses are recovered through future high-quality opportunities—not martingale, revenge trading or reckless sizing.

11. **Risk Is Relative to Opportunity**
    Capital allocation depends on expected edge, confidence, volatility, liquidity, drawdown and current portfolio state—not crude fixed trade-count rules.

12. **Execution Quality Matters**
    A good prediction is worthless if spread, slippage, charges or liquidity destroy the edge. Rank opportunities by expected **net** return after realistic trading costs.

13. **Self-Learning Is Mandatory**
    Learn from both executed and rejected opportunities. Measure what subsequently happened, identify missed opportunities and bad filters, propose improvements, validate them, then promote only proven changes.

14. **Keep Architecture Lean**
    One canonical data layer, decision engine, execution engine, risk engine, ledger and learning loop. No duplicate runtimes, competing paper engines or unnecessary complexity.

15. **Reuse Before Rebuilding**
    Leverage proven libraries, market data, existing infrastructure, published strategies, external intelligence and validated components. Custom engineering should focus on information fusion, prediction, ranking, capital allocation and learning.

16. **Always-Free First**
    Architecture must remain within free/always-free resources wherever feasible. Avoid paid infrastructure or data dependencies unless explicitly approved. Use OCI/GitHub/Upstox/free sources efficiently.

## Daybagger Decision Flow

ALL AVAILABLE INTELLIGENCE
→ MARKET REGIME
→ MULTIPLE SPECIALIST MODELS
→ PREDICTIVE META-ENGINE
→ EXPECTED-VALUE RANKING
→ BEST EQUITY OPPORTUNITIES
→ CAPITAL & RISK ALLOCATION
→ EXECUTION
→ P&L / OUTCOME
→ CONTINUOUS EVIDENCE-BASED LEARNING

## Ultimate Principle

**Daybagger must become smarter from evidence, not more complicated from patches.**
